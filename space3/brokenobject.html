The Object Was Broken, Yet The Friendship Wasn't

date Random

To be honest, I can be a clumsy person at times—and many occasions have proven it right. I stumble and get hit by random objects almost every day, even in situations where it seems nearly impossible. At often times, I also accidentally drop things, spill drinks, and even break stuffs. Not too long ago, I dropped the last piece of donut I was saving to eat, huhu. Surely, that was quite painful, but it was alright. It’s mine anyway, I was only responsible to control my feeling and reaction about it—so, it’s nothing compared to damaging something of someone else’s. Well, you know, I’m mentioning this because I’ve felt it before.

***

The smile on my friend’s face vanished drastically as she saw it—a trophy of hers had broken. Seeing her expression, I can’t help but feeling guilty. Yes, I was the one who fortuitously broke it and I admitted it to her right away. At that moment, even I could tell that she was struggling to process what was happening. It was in the middle of an afternoon free time at middle school, and she happened to have some business out of the classroom.

In the meantime, our other friends and I were just chilling in the classroom—around her desk, to be precise. In the drawer of her desk, there was several objects she kept there, including an acrylic/glass-like trophy. As I remember, I checked on that trophy probably to see where’d she got it from, then somehow, I accidentally dropped it. For a second there, I was really hoping that the trophy was alright. But unfortunately, I had to grasp to the fact that it was broken into two or three pieces. I instantly became terrified but could do nothing about it except apologizing to her sincerely—and I obviously did as she came back to our classroom.

If I remember it correctly, she started tearing up a moment after finding out it was broken. I knew she was likely in the middle of something when she’s out of the class, hence finding her valued belonging broken as she arrived to the class must have felt heartbreaking. At the very moment, I felt at a complete guilt; it’s like I was crying inside and I couldn’t wait to go home so that I could calm myself down a bit.

Moreover, another friend of mine said something which unintentionally added fuel to my guilty feeling. She basically said that it’s different when we break it ourselves compared to if someone else breaks it. To me, it sounded like she’s trying to tell me that my friend who owns the trophy must feel really bad because it was another person who broke her trophy; had she broken it herself, she wouldn’t have been that sad. Hearing that just worsened my feeling since I agreed with that too.

That day was passing really slow and there was no more conversation between me and my friend. Our class ended around Ashar prayer, so as usual, we went to the school mosque first before really going home. After the prayer, to my surprise, that friend whose trophy I broke came up to me. She said sorry for making me feel bad and hugged me. Even more, she explained why she reacted that way, mentioning she was in the middle of something as well.

I was genuinely touched by her actions. It was me who made a mistake, but she apologized to me too, like why? Anyway, I’m so thankful that she did that. Otherwise, I would’ve spent a long time contemplating on what to do to make up with her. Following that wholesome moment, everything went back to normal. To this day and ‘till forever, she’s one of the best sweetest friends of mine. I guess I need a lesson of how to face and fix social problems like her—as well as learning to be more careful next time.

Ps: You know what, I even had notes on my phone about this incident saying that I want to go home soon, how sorry I was, and even quoted what the other friend of mine said.

Oh and by the way, I broke a broom during my campus trip about one or two months ago and I was reminded of this moment again :).
