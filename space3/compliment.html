A Compliment That Stays

July 19, 2025 Random

We may not always realize, but compliments affect us in many ways—and those effects are more on the positive side, both for the receiver and the giver. The act of giving compliments is known to boost moods and strengthen the relationship between the two parties. Besides, compliments are obviously useful to uplift self-esteem and confidence of the one receiving them.

Furthermore, receiving compliments often encourage and motivate us to strive even more for the better. A compliment is normally based on subjective opinion or judgement by other people—it most possibly resonates with the truth, but not always. But even if it doesn’t, I suppose there’s no problem with that. In fact, the compliment itself may become a key motivation for the receiver to make the compliment coming true. To me, that just proves how powerful a single compliment could be and I can say that I’m fully aware of that. Personally, I think I’ve received some compliments which are quietly impactful to my personal growth. So, I want to talk about a compliment I once received and has stayed in my thoughts for long.

Welcome to another recount story by me. By the way, beware, I guess might make me a little full of myself, hehe. So at the end of my first middle school term, my school organized a kind of end-term event where every class was competing each other in arts, sports, and academic type of competitions. Every class assigns their members for each competition—and I was assigned for one of those too, by the way. Anyway, although it was just an internal event and we were merely competing our fellow friends from other classes, the hype around the competitions were surprisingly high, including for the one I participated in.

Thankfully, I didn’t let my classmates down; I won it! Then, after all the events of the day ended, some friends of mine were still talking about it—basically about anything that happened that day. At some points as we were having the conversation, I also expressed things I noticed during the event, including my opinion about some behaviours of others.

Little did I know, everything I said lead me to hear a compliment I didn’t expect. Instead of commenting about the event, a friend of mine complimented me by saying that I have a decent psychological observation or something like that. I probably don’t need to mention how I felt, but let me tell you: honestly, I was flattered. To hear someone pointing out a subtle quality that I wasn’t even sure I had felt like another level of compliment. I’ve forgotten how I responded to hear, but I guess in my head I was like, “Oh wow, thanks, didn’t know I’d love to hear that!”

In reality, I probably just grinned or chuckled then then moved on that time, but deep down inside I cherish it so much. It’s been years after that, and that one compliment stays in my head. That one thing my friend said possibly brings out more confidence in me than I could ever realize—made me want to realize that as well. Thanks to the compliment, I now lowkey believe I can read mind, haha.

As a closure to this piece, I wanna say this: sometimes, the compliment you least or never expected, turns out to be one of the best things you’ve ever heard.
