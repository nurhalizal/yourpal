My Earliest Piece of Memory

July 17, 2025 Random

I find it a bit strange to think of how people gain consciousness during early stages of life. It seems to me that being a baby is so fun, and it’s sad that I don’t remember anything from the time I was a baby. Sometimes, I stupidly wish I had been a fully conscious baby so I could have told myself to remember all those good moments back then. But I’m sure that we’re meant to grow this way—gaining consciousness and creating memories eventually—for a reason.

Speaking of memory and initial consciousness, I like to think about what my earliest memories were about. Turned out, it’s quite difficult to determine which one of the childhood memories I had was the earliest. I can’t even tell when each of them happened. Fortunately, my parents, especially my father took some pictures of me as a little child. They even turned some of the photos into one small album which was given to me when I was four. I happened to know that it was given when I was four, because the album seemed to have several then recently taken pictures of my 4th birthday.

Even so, evidently those 4th birthday photos were not exactly my earliest memory. I saw other images of me which I believe were taken even earlier—including my 3rd birthday pictures. So basically, my way of recalling my earliest memories is considering which of my childhood photos I remember the moments of them being taken and among those, which one could possibly be the earliest. Honestly, I don’t even know how I came up with this, but I concluded that my earliest memory is my 3rd birthday. Although I don’t recall entirely about the birthday party, there are some particular details that I do remember. For instances, from the photos, I recognize well the clothes I was wearing, the birthday hats, the cake, and even which of the wrapped gifts was from my brother. It’s just that I have no idea how that party was going on. But I suppose those details were enough for me to deduce that it was indeed my earliest memory.

Aside of it, I also have a vague yet vivid memory of my father taking some pictures of me (what’d you mean vague yet vivid, though?). Those photos were basically just me posing randomly while holding some small toys like a ball I don’t even know where I got that from. And I believe, that those pics were taken when I was three, so still after my third birthday. In conclusion, my 3rd birthday party stands still as my earliest memory, yay.

By the way, thinking of all this right now somehow reminds me of how loved I’ve been my whole life. Even with the fact that I have no idea what was happening most of the time during my early years of life, the ones that I remember—and those photos—just show how great my life has been and wonderful my childhood was.
