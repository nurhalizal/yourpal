How I Hate Cheating

July 23, 2025 Random

In one of my recent pieces, I mentioned that I’m against cheating, as I believe everyone should. By the way, just want to point out that here I’m focusing about cheating in class, like during an exam and so on. In this case, I’m convinced that I’ve always hated cheating ever since I knew about the existence of such behaviour. But that doesn’t mean I’m all clear about it. I have a vivid memory of a moment I assume myself cheating in an exam—which I regret to this day. As I always love to do, I’m gonna recount the exact moment of it.

It happened when I was in elementary school—yeah, I was just a little kid. We had an exam today, so I spent my time studying before it started—I read my books and notes a few times, trying to get the best understanding of the materials and remember important stuffs of it. As the exam was about to begin, I started tidying up my books and notes. I put them back into my bag to make sure I wouldn’t get accused of cheating.

Before I was finished tidying my stuffs up, my teacher had started writing the exam problems/questions on the whiteboard. Of course, I instinctively read it. But then as I read the first number, I realized I forgot what’s the exact answer/solution to it—it’s like I know that I would’ve known that or have read about it, yet I was completely perplexed. I was thinking, if only I could get a little clue, I would be able to answer it nicely. Eventually, my ‘evil’ thought seemed to take over—I ended up pulling a book back out of my bag, and got the clue that I needed. Long story short, I completed the exam and got away with it.

Yeah, I didn’t get in trouble at all about it, and if you think of it from another point of view, the exam didn’t technically start yet when I pulled out and read my book for a split second. But in spite of it, I couldn’t help but feeling guilty about it for a long time. I happen to accuse myself as cheating that day and the guilt about it has haunted me this whole time.

That moment really hit me inside—like a kick of realization that cheating is indeed that bad. I had merely a few seconds re-reading a piece of note before starting the exam, yet it already felt so wrong. To this day, that moment along with the guilt it had given me still flashes before my eyes almost every time the word ‘cheating’ came to my head—even if it’s just by realizing that there are other people who are committing it. It’s been a long time and I don’t even think this guilty feeling will ever vanish entirely. But thanks to the guilt from that moment; I think it has helped me prevent cheating at all cost.

Anyway, it’s sad that I ‘see’ some people have normalized the act of cheating nowadays. Nevertheless, cheating is against basic moral or principle in any cases and we know that. However, knowing that things are bad don’t always work for us to avoid ‘em right away and entirely. Sometimes, we need a certain hit of realization that it is indeed bad.
